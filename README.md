## The entry

> Pick up the phone that Tuesday evening in March when Grandma calls, because she will not call again tomorrow.
> You let it ring to voicemail and meant to call back, and the goodbye you kept saving for tomorrow never got said.

## Why this moment

The moment was chosen because it can be pictured in a single image: a phone ringing on one ordinary Tuesday evening in March, and a younger self letting it go to voicemail while assuming tomorrow is guaranteed. The consequence was chosen because it follows directly and irreversibly from that one missed ring — the goodbye the writer kept saving for tomorrow never got said, because there was no tomorrow for that call. The warning sets up the moment and the second line lands what changed because of it, in a plain voice addressed to one specific person rather than to an audience.
